Vulkan or ROCm? Speed and energy per token on one mini PC

Speed and energy per token for four local models on one AMD Ryzen AI MAX+ 395 mini PC, measured at the wall plug and at the chip, on two software backends, with the plan and the expectations written down before the run.

Self-published. Not peer reviewed. One machine.

What I found

  • Speed. ROCm decoded more slowly than Vulkan in 16 of 16 cells, by 1 to 12%, and the repeat ranges of the two backends never overlap in those cells. It was never faster.
  • Energy, 9B dense. ROCm used more energy per token than Vulkan: +8 to +20% across the four context lengths (wall plug, net of idle).
  • Energy, 12B dense. ROCm used about the same (the repeat ranges overlap in some cells) energy per token than Vulkan: -6 to +3% across the four context lengths (wall plug, net of idle).
  • Energy, 26B mixture. ROCm used about the same (the repeat ranges overlap in some cells) energy per token than Vulkan: -6 to -1% across the four context lengths (wall plug, net of idle).
  • Energy, 35B mixture. ROCm used less energy per token than Vulkan: -8 to -6% across the four context lengths (wall plug, net of idle).
  • Repeatability. Vulkan on the newer kernel, on another day, matched study 1 within 1.52% in every one of the 16 cells. Idle power was 17 W against 16.5 W.

What this does not show

  • Anything about other hardware, other chips, discrete GPUs or other builds of llama.cpp.
  • The accuracy of the wall watts. The plug is uncalibrated and reads whole watts. The chip sensor is what the chip reports about itself.
  • Batched or concurrent serving. Everything here is one stream at a time.
  • Quality of the output. Prompts are licence texts and the replies are not scored.
  • That anyone outside this project has repeated it.
